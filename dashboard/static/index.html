What each panel means

Header
Where lines come from, whether the engine is still calibrating its baseline (the first N lines define "normal") or live, uptime, and the current distance threshold. Pause freezes the view without stopping the engine.
KPI cards
Throughput over the last 60 s, totals since the engine (re)started, anomalies and their share, and lines no parser format matched.
Throughput
Per-second line and anomaly counts. Spikes of red during a burst are attacks being caught as they happen.
Severity
The KNN classifier's prediction. It learns from keyword-based seed labels on a sliding window and is refit every 100 lines.
Anomaly distance
Each dot is a line's mean distance to its k nearest neighbours in the (standardized) baseline. Above the dashed threshold = anomalous. The threshold is a percentile of the baseline's own distances.
Top offending IPs
Sources of the most failed logins recently: brute-force candidates.
Live log
Every event, colour-coded by severity; anomalies are tinted red with the reason (which features stood out, in standard deviations). Log text is attacker-controlled, so it is always inserted as plain text.
Detail drawer
Raw line, parsed fields, the feature vector fed to the models, and distance versus threshold.
Live evaluation
Only for synthetic traffic, where every line carries a ground-truth label: precision, recall, F1 and recall per anomaly type.
